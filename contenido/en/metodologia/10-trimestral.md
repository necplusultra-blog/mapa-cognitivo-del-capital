## Quarterly estimate

Numerator: quarterly profits from NIPA 1.14, at a seasonally adjusted annual rate. Denominator: net stock of fixed capital at current cost, linearly interpolated between year-ends:

<div class="f">K<sub>a,t</sub> = K<sub>a−1</sub> + (K<sub>a</sub> − K<sub>a−1</sub>) · t/4</div>

After the last published year, K is extrapolated with the growth of the last year; those quarters are marked as provisional (dotted segment).
