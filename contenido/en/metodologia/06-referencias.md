## The six readings in the debate

The cognitive map sets six currents of the Marxist debate to read the same data. For five of them it calculates a rate ‘in the manner of’ their authors; the sixth, Heinrich's (2013; 2014), does not propose a measure and is contrasted with the full spread of the multiverse.

| Reading | Measure that represents it | Specification |
|---|---|---|
| Shaikh (and Tonak) | Broad surplus value by industry | variant b: business value added − compensation in the productive industries, over K |
| Moseley | Broad surplus value within-industry | variant c (from 1972): only the compensation of production and non-supervisory workers is subtracted |
| Kliman | Corporate, historical cost | corporate net surplus over K at historical cost |
| Duménil and Lévy | Non-financial, current cost | net surplus of non-financial corporations over K at current cost |
| Roberts and Carchedi | pv/(c + v) and world rate | variant b with annual variable capital in the denominator; world net rate from the Penn World Table |
| Heinrich | (none) | the spread of the multiverse and the table of robustness by decade |

To these is added a baseline, **business, all productive** (variant a), which does not distinguish between productive and unproductive labour.

All of them are net, include intellectual property, include neither inventories nor a utilisation adjustment, and use all capital. They are definitions ‘in the manner of’ the authors; the replication of their series, with each author's own definition and current data, is in the [next section](#replicacion). The diagnoses the site attributes to each current summarise their published theses; the critical reading of all six is that of the doctoral research and is in the [Reading](lectura.html#seis-lecturas).
