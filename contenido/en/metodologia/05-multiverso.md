## The multiverse of specifications

The main multiverse (1948–) combines these forks and adds up to {{m-n}} specifications. Each one is normalised to its own average for the period (=100) and the site shows its 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles.

| Fork | Options | Associated position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sector | total corporate · non-financial corporate · business sector | — |
| Numerator (corporate) | net operating surplus · profit before tax · profit after tax | Duménil-Lévy, Kliman |
| Numerator (business) | broad surplus (all productive) · broad surplus value by industry · broad surplus value within-industry (separate family, from 1972) | Shaikh-Tonak, Moseley |
| Capital valuation | current cost · historical cost | Duménil-Lévy, Roberts · Kliman |
| Net or gross | net of depreciation · gross (profit + depreciation over net stock) | — |
| Intellectual property | inside K · outside K (treated as an expense: profit − investment in IPP + its depreciation) | debate on the value transfer of IPP |
| Inventories | without · with inventories (Z.1) in K | — |
| Capacity utilisation | no adjustment · r × (mean u / u) | — |
| Denominator (business) | K: fixed capital advanced · K + V: fixed capital plus variable capital, pv/(c + v) | Marx's definition of the rate of profit |
| Scope of K (business) | all non-residential capital · productive industries only (excludes trade, finance and insurance, legal services, non-residential real estate and rental and leasing) | Moseley, Shaikh-Tonak |

**Scope of K.** With ‘productive industries only’, the capital of the industries that §3 declares unproductive leaves the denominator and its depreciation is no longer subtracted from the numerator: like the wages of those industries, it is paid out of surplus value (consistency with the by-industry criterion; correction from the November 2026 audit). If intellectual property is already outside K, only equipment and structures are removed from those industries, so as not to subtract their intellectual property twice.

**Denominator.** The rate of profit is defined as pv/(c + v), surplus value over the total capital advanced. In the K + V fork, v is the annual compensation of productive labour in the same variant (a, b or c). Using the annual flow amounts to assuming a single turnover of variable capital per year: if v turns over n times, the variable capital advanced is v/n and the rate lies between the two published variants (K and K + V). The national accounts do not publish the number of turnovers. The fork exists only in the business sector, where v is defined; in the corporate specifications the denominator is K.

K is always the BEA's net stock of fixed assets (end of year). In the business sector, non-profit institutions and households are excluded. The full list of specifications, with all their series, is in the [downloads](descargas/) (`eeuu_especificaciones.csv`, `eeuu_multiverso_tasas.csv`).
