## Endogenous breaks

Piecewise linear trends are fitted to each specification (without imposed continuity between segments, as in Bai and Perron, 1998; 2003), with a minimum segment length of h years and up to 5 breaks. The number of breaks is chosen by information criterion:

<div class="f">BIC = n·ln(RSS/n) + k·ln(n) · LWZ = n·ln(RSS/n) + 0.299·k·(ln n)<sup>2.1</sup> · k = 3m + 2</div>

Break dates are never fixed in advance. The chart counts how many specifications detect a break in each year (baseline configuration: h = 8, BIC). The sensitivity table repeats the calculation with h = 6, 10 and with the LWZ criterion (Liu, Wu and Zidek, 1997).
