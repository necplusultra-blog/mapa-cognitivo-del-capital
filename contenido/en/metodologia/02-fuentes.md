## Sources and frequency

- **BEA** (Bureau of Economic Analysis): annual and quarterly national accounts (files `NipaDataA`, `NipaDataQ`), fixed assets (`FixedAssets`), and GDP by industry (SIC 1947–1986, NAICS 1987–1996, NAICS 1997–).
- **Federal Reserve**, via FRED: inventories of non-financial corporations (Z.1, `BOGZ1FL105020005A`) and manufacturing capacity utilisation (G.17, `CUMFNS`).
- **BLS** (Bureau of Labor Statistics), CES survey via FRED: employment, weekly hours and hourly earnings of production and non-supervisory workers, by supersector.
- **Penn World Table 11.0** (Feenstra, Inklaar and Timmer, 2015): labour share, capital, depreciation, internal rate of return and GDP for 185 countries, 1950–2023.
- **OECD**, annual national accounts by industry (tables 6 and 7, from 2014). **ILO** (ILOSTAT): labour share, employees, employment and hours. India's **NSS and PLFS** surveys: distribution of the employed by status in employment.
- **WIOD** (Timmer et al., 2015), Socio-Economic Accounts 2016 and national input–output tables: value added, compensation, employees and hours by industry, for 43 countries, 2000–2014.
- **World Bank**: GDP in current dollars.
- **SEC**: XBRL financial statements of the AI corporations.

The site is recalculated on the 3rd of each month and whenever the code changes. The figures change when the sources publish: the BEA every quarter and in its annual revision in September, which may revise the entire history; the PWT every few years. Earlier versions of the results remain in the repository history.
