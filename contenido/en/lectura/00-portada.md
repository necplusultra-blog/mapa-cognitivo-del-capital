# A first reading of the results

What the seismograph's first measurement shows when it is read from the standpoint of the theory of the immanent limits of capital. The aim is to give an account of the fundamental tendencies of contemporary capitalist production: what form the rate and mass of surplus value, exploitation, the composition of capital and the field of its countertendencies take today; how the main Marxist currents read them; and what objective possibilities the development of the productive forces contains. This is not a summary of figures but an interpretation: the figures are those of the November 2026 measurement and may change when the sources publish new data.
{: .lede }

Interpretation developed within the doctoral research of Pablo Ignacio Jiménez Cea, *Los límites inmanentes del capital* (SECIHTI — Mexico’s Secretariat of Science, Humanities, Technology and Innovation — scholarship number 4052073), and of his article ‘The immanent dialectic of capital’ (*Capital & Class*, 2026). Preliminary version, drafted with the assistance of artificial intelligence on the basis of the author’s theoretical framework.
