## Critique of political economy, not critical political economy

This work seeks to take up and continue the necessarily unfinished project of Marx's critique of political economy. Unfinished, because Marx left Volumes II and III of *Capital* as incomplete manuscripts, along with manuscripts and drafts of the project of a critique of political economy, written before and after his magnum opus, that run to thousands of pages. It is a necessarily unfinished project, moreover, because its object is not a fixed structure but a historically specific movement of social production that continually transforms the world while reconstituting the relations of production immanent to it: the immanent critique of capitalist society must therefore continue that project by studying the fundamental tendencies of contemporary capitalism.

### The qualitative comes first

There is a difference of principle between the critique of political economy and a critical political economy. The latter takes the categories of economics —profit, wage, capital— as given transhistorical forms, while it discusses their measurement or their quantitative distribution. The former, by contrast, asks why the product of labour takes the form of the commodity, of value and of capital. For it, these categories are forms of a historically specific social relation: socially valid, and therefore objective, forms of thought, which impose themselves on individuals as if they were natural properties of things (Marx, 2018, pp. 91–93). Value, surplus value and capital are not, in this sense, quantities to be found immediately in official economic statistics. They are categories of a determinate mode of social relations, of social praxis, which correspond to the fundamental relations of production of capitalist society. They are consequently categories of the critique of political economy because, as the immanent critique of capitalist society, it gives an account of those fundamental relations and reveals the categories of political economy to be necessary forms of appearance of those social relations and practices on the surface of society.

That reality has, as Dr Alfonso García Vela has pointed out to me, a quantitative dimension of enormous force. Value exists only as a category of the totality and, as abstract wealth grounded in labour time, as a determinate quantitative magnitude: as socially necessary labour time, as money, as the rate of profit, and so on. Yet if measurement were to become independent of the critical category, the critique would relapse into mere Marxist economics: the rate of profit becomes a thermometer whose trajectory would by itself decide the fate of capitalism. This is the question shared, though they give opposite answers to it, by Robert Kurz's breakdown reading and by Michael Heinrich's reading of the indeterminacy of the tendential fall of the rate of profit. The cognitive map seeks to avoid that relapse through four rules:

1. **The categories of the critique of political economy decide what is counted.** Which labour is productive, what enters capital, whether households' payments to finance are surplus value: none of these questions is settled statistically. They are theoretical and categorial decisions, and the site makes them explicit at each fork.
2. **Statistical series show; they do not demonstrate.** They record the phenomenal manifestation of fundamental determinations of capital set out by Marxian theory; they neither prove those determinations nor measure the distance to any limit.
3. **Measuring in time is part of the critique.** The cognitive map expresses wealth in hours of labour not because time is a natural measure of wealth, but because it is the measure that capital imposes on it. What is measured is the object of the critique, not a neutral yardstick.
4. **What is decisive are the transformations and tendencies of contemporary capitalism.** The transformations that matter —secondary exploitation through finance, artificial intelligence as a general condition of production, the disposable time that capital turns into surplus labour— are changes in the qualitative articulation of the field of countertendencies. The figures serve to recognise the empirical way in which the fundamental tendencies of capitalist production manifest themselves, not as a substitute for their immanent critique.

### Why use artificial intelligence

Marx did not elaborate the concept of capital through the abstractions of his thought alone. The concept of capital in Marx is the result of a long theoretical practice, full of discoveries, dead ends, abandoned drafts and the continual concretisation of the categories. To that end he processed an enormous quantity of empirical material: the reports of the factory inspectors and the blue books of the British Parliament on which the chapter on the working day in *Capital* rests (Marx, 2018), the notebooks in which he recorded the world crisis of 1857 day by day (Marx, 2017) and, in the 1870s, extensive studies of the agrarian statistics of Russia and the United States, of banking and of crises (Heinrich, 2013). That empirical work did not illustrate a critical category elaborated in advance: it was part of his elaboration and continual concretisation of the concept.

Today the official sources —national accounts, input–output tables, employment and hours surveys from dozens of countries over more than seventy years— exceed by several orders of magnitude what a researcher could process by hand. Artificial intelligence makes it possible to gather, harmonise and recompute them, and to leave every step in view so that anyone can audit it. With it, the continuation of Marx's theoretical project can be combined with the study of how the fundamental tendencies of capital are expressed empirically in the material development of capitalist society, drawing on the largest possible quantity of data and official sources.

The tool, however, decides nothing. The labour of the concept and metacognition remain human. The categories, the criteria and the theoretical interpretation of the data are mine, and the code and the decisions are public so that anyone can test the evidence, enrich it or point out possible errors in my analysis. There is, moreover, a reflexivity that should not be lost from view: artificial intelligence is one of the productive forces whose capitalist form this very cognitive map analyses. Using it for the critique means employing a power of the social intellect against the form in which it is presented to society: as capital.
