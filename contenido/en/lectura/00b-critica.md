## Critique of political economy, not critical political economy

This work seeks to take up and continue the necessarily unfinished project of the Marxian critique of political economy. Unfinished, because Marx left Books II and III of *Capital* as manuscripts and never wrote the books he had planned on the state, foreign trade and the world market. Necessarily unfinished, because its object is not a fixed structure but a historical movement that reconstitutes itself again and again on new material foundations: the critique has to follow it in the forms it adopts today.

### The qualitative comes first

There is a difference of principle between the critique of political economy and a critical political economy. The latter takes the categories of economics —profit, wages, capital— as given magnitudes and debates their measurement or their distribution. The former asks why the product of labour takes the form of commodity, of value and of capital. For it, these categories are forms of a historically specific social relation: socially valid and therefore objective forms of thought, which impose themselves on individuals as if they were natural properties of things (Marx, 2018, ch. I, § 4). Value, surplus value and capital are not quantities to be found in statistics. They are conceptual determinations of a social relation that really exists, and that is why capitalist society is, at its foundations, a conceptual reality.

This reality has a quantitative dimension of enormous force, because value exists only as proportion: as socially necessary labour time, as money, as a rate of profit that decides what is produced and what is not. But if measurement becomes independent of the concept, the critique relapses into Marxist economics: the rate of profit becomes a thermometer whose trajectory would by itself decide the fate of capitalism. This is the question shared —even if they answer it in opposite ways— by the breakdown readings and the indeterminacy readings. The cognitive map tries to avoid that relapse with four rules:

1. **The concept decides what is counted.** Which labour is productive, what enters into capital, whether household payments to finance are surplus value: none of these questions is settled statistically. They are conceptual decisions, and the site makes them explicit at every fork.
2. **The series show; they do not demonstrate.** They record the phenomenal manifestation of determinations that the theory sets out; they neither prove those determinations nor measure the distance to any limit.
3. **Measuring in time is part of the critique.** The cognitive map expresses wealth in hours of labour not because time is a natural measure of wealth, but because it is the measure that capital imposes on it. What is measured is the object of the critique, not a neutral yardstick.
4. **What is decisive are the changes of form.** The transformations that matter —secondary exploitation by finance, artificial intelligence as a general condition of production, the disposable time that capital converts into surplus labour— are changes in the qualitative articulation of the field of countertendencies. The figures serve to recognise them; they do not replace them.

### Why artificial intelligence

Marx did not develop the concept of capital through logic alone. During his lifetime he processed an enormous quantity of empirical material: the reports of the factory inspectors and the Blue Books of the British Parliament that underpin the chapter on the working day (Marx, 2018, ch. VIII), the notebooks in which he recorded the world crisis of 1857 day by day (Marx, 2017) and, in the 1870s, extensive studies of the agrarian statistics of Russia and the United States, of banking and of crises (Heinrich, 2013). That empirical work did not illustrate a ready-made concept: it was part of its elaboration.

Today, official sources —national accounts, input–output tables, employment and hours surveys from dozens of countries, covering more than seventy years— exceed by several orders of magnitude what a researcher can process by hand. Artificial intelligence makes it possible to gather, harmonise and recalculate them, and to leave every step in view so that anyone can audit it. With it, it becomes possible to amalgamate the continuation of the Marxian theoretical project with the study of how the fundamental tendencies of capital are empirically expressed in the material development of capitalist society, on the basis of the greatest possible quantity of data and official sources.

The tool decides nothing. The categories, the criteria and the interpretation are the author’s, and the code and the decisions are public. There is, moreover, a reflexivity that should not be lost sight of: artificial intelligence is one of the productive forces whose capitalist form the cognitive map itself analyses (Dyer-Witheford, Kjøsen and Steinhoff, 2024). To use it for the critique is to employ a power of the social intellect against the form in which it presents itself to society: as capital.
