## Scope and sources

The data show the phenomenal manifestation of categorial determinations; they do not demonstrate them. What can be determined is not a distance to a ceiling, but the way in which the field of countertendencies is articulated.

Sources: BEA (national accounts, fixed assets, GDP by industry), Federal Reserve (Z.1, G.17), BLS (CES), Penn World Table 11.0, WIOD, OECD, ILO, NSS/PLFS (India) and SEC. The site is recalculated on the 3rd of each month; the data change when the sources publish.

All series can be [downloaded as CSV](descargas/). Formulas, theoretical decisions, splices and limitations: [methodology](metodologia.html).
