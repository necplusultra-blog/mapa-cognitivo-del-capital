## Decisions of the multiverse

There is no single rate of profit: each specification results from a combination of explicit theoretical decisions. These are the forks of the two trees on the map, with their options and the currents that usually take them. Their justification is in [Methodology §5](metodologia.html#multiverso); on the home page, the [explorer](index.html#explorador) lets you choose an option at each fork and see the resulting curve.
