## Decisions of the set of calculations of the rate of profit

There is no single way of measuring the rate of profit across the different currents of contemporary Marxist theory: each specification results from a combination of explicit theoretical decisions. These are the forks of the two decision trees on the map, with their options and the currents that usually take them. Their justification is in [Methodology §5](metodologia.html#multiverso); on the home page, the [explorer](index.html#explorador) lets you choose an option at each fork and see the resulting curve.
