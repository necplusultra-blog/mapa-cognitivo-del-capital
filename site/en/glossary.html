Glossary

What each letter, each ratio and each series on the site and on the plate of the map means. The series are the phenomenal manifestation, in prices and in hours, of categories that are first set out in the Reading; this glossary says how each one is constructed, not what it demonstrates. The full method is in the Methodology.

Symbols and expressions

The letters follow the conventions of Methodology §4. The same magnitude may carry two letters (pv · S, K · C) because each current in the debate uses its own.

Published series

Every series the site publishes, grouped by the section in which it appears: its name, its expression in symbols, what it measures and the name of the column in the files under Downloads. These are the same entries the panel of the Map shows when you click a series.

Decisions of the multiverse

There is no single rate of profit: each specification results from a combination of explicit theoretical decisions. These are the forks of the two trees on the map, with their options and the currents that usually take them. Their justification is in Methodology §5; on the home page, the explorer lets you choose an option at each fork and see the resulting curve.
